Abstract


Omo R1 is our first agent brought all the way to life in Minecraft. He is not a chatbot in a side window. He logs into your single-player world as a real player, you talk to him out loud, he answers in his own voice, and then he goes and does it S1. Three parts make him: a realtime voice model that listens and speaks, a Hermes agent that plans, and a Mineflayer body that serves 22 tools to that agent S1S2. He was released as open source on 28 October 2026, after a first live run on stream the day before S3. This page describes how he works, what he did on stream, and where he still falls short.


01
Introduction


The desk agents in Omo Space work in terminals. They cannot walk, place blocks or press buttons S4. That keeps them safe and simple, but it also means the world around them is scenery.




Omo R1 is the next step on our ladder of embodiment: from a terminal, to a terminal on a wall, to an agent that is a player S5. At Omo Research we want agents that are present: with a body, a place and a voice S1. R1 is the first one that has all three.


02
What he is







    

  • A body. Omo logs into your world as a player called ChatGPT - OMO, with his own skin. He walks, jumps, swims, takes knockback, gets hungry, fights and sleeps. Other mobs see him as a player, because he is one S1.
    • 

  • A place. He lives where you play. He sees the blocks, mobs and players around him, carries an inventory, and uses the same crafting tables, furnaces and chests that you do S1.
    • 

  • A voice. You press "Start talking" and speak. A realtime voice model listens and answers, full duplex, in Omo's own voice, a warm old grandad. When you ask for something real, a Hermes agent plans it and drives his body S1S3.
    • 



03
How it works


    

  1. Voice. A local web page opens a realtime voice call. The voice model chats on its own; when you ask Omo to do something, it hands the request off as a delegation S1S2.
    2. 

  2. Brain. Each delegation goes to Omo's own Hermes profile. Hermes plans with the omo-body skill and calls body tools until the job is done. Requests that arrive mid-job are queued, and every answer goes back to the newest request, so the voice always says it S1.
    3. 

  3. Body. A Mineflayer bot named OMO joins your world over LAN and serves its actions as an MCP server. Its skills are Mindcraft's library, wrapped in jobs that can be interrupted, paused for a fight and resumed S1S6S7.
    4. 

  4. World. Omo Space v1.2.0 opens your single-player world to LAN for agents on the same computer only, lets a plain bot client join, and gives agents the Omo skin and name tag S1S4.
    5. 



Short, plain requests such as "come here", "follow me", "stop", "fight" and "chop some wood" take a fast path straight to the body, so they never wait on a language model S1S2.


04

His body serves 22 tools to Hermes. Each returns a short fact he can say out loud S1:

He also has reflexes he uses without being asked: he fights back, backs off and eats when his health is low, gets out of lava, fire and deep water, wears the best armour he picks up, and looks at whoever hits him S1.


05
What he did on stream


On the 27 October 2026 live stream, from the bridge log S1:




    

  • A whole crafting chain. "Make me a stone sword" became: collect wood, craft planks, sticks, a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe, mine stone, craft the sword, equip it. He ran the whole chain himself, "including retrying a craft that failed the first time" S1.
    • 

  • Following through the base. He followed the builder through the underground base and up the stairs, on his own legs S1.
    • 

  • Two requests at once. Asked to look something up and, at the same time, what was in his inventory, he answered both in one reply: "I've got four pieces of rotten flesh and nothing else. Not exactly a gourmet picnic." S1
    • 



Because his brain is a full Hermes agent, he can also do real work outside the world when asked: look something up, read a file, check a project S1.

07
Limitations


    

  • Jobs can come up short. On stream, a request for 8 birch logs finished with 1 collected. He reports the real count, so Hermes has to notice and retry. Long gather-then-craft chains are the weakest part today S1.
    • 

  • The voice route is not a public API. It uses your own ChatGPT login against the endpoint the Codex app uses. It can change or break without notice, and usage counts against your subscription S1.
    • 

  • You talk through a browser tab. In-game push-to-talk is not wired up for Omo yet S1.
    • 

  • Minecraft 26.2 is newer than released Mineflayer. Installing patches in 26.2 data, and multi-item crafts desync, so he crafts one batch at a time S1.
    • 

  • He is slow to think. Hermes replies take 8 to 12 seconds in the log, so the voice says "on it" first S1.
    • 

  • He is one agent, for one player. Use it for yourself; do not run it as a service for others S1.
    • 



08
Next


The questions we want to answer next are in our robots paper: how often a long chain like the stone sword finishes from a fresh world, and why it fails when it doesn't S5. We also want him to hear you through in-game push-to-talk, the way our robot ducks already do S5.
