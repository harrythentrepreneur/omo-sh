Research into the next interface

Writing down the question that became Omo Research.

When

Jun 2026

Theme

Writing

Status

Note

Log

8 of 51

01 / What it was

On 22 June a phone note got a title and almost nothing else: “Research into the next interface of AI and agents”.

A week later, a longer note put the question plainly: the chat window is not the future of working with agents, so what is? It sketched a film in which you walk through a Minecraft world, and agents work in rooms you can visit.

04 / Where it led

The question became the name and the line of the omo.sh site in July.
