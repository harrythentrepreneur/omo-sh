03 / Status

Notes before papers

Editorial, not empirical

A useful idea should be clear about what it knows.

Omo has not published research papers or controlled studies. These notes organize questions, interpret public work, and expose assumptions early enough to be challenged. The research log records the interfaces we have built and used, and will say plainly when later experiments support or overturn an idea.
