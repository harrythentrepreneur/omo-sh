Typography

Rigid grids are for measuring. Wind is for moving.
Yifan (逸凡) — free and unspirited, like wind that cannot be contained by any structure. The Wind variation sets flowing cobalt lines against a rigid monochrome grid. The "O" mark is deliberately broken open — a circle that refuses to close — allowing the wind lines to pass through instead of being blocked. Every flow line is rendered as segmented geometric polylines, keeping the brutalist DNA while suggesting motion. Cobalt (#0038a8) appears only in the wind lines and nowhere else.
All typography: IBM Plex Mono (300–700)  |  Accent: Cobalt Wind #0038a8

Style Rationale

The Yifan Wind direction applies 逸凡 as flow moving through structure rather than breaking it. A rigid 20px grid — the brutalist drafting skeleton — underlies the entire logo mark. The double-frame remains intact, but the "O" circle is deliberately opened at its bottom edge so that cobalt wind lines (#0038a8) can sweep through. These lines are rendered as polyline segments — geometric, angular, unmistakably brutalist — yet they curve across the composition like air. The palette is B&W with five structural grays plus a single cobalt accent reserved exclusively for motion. This direction says: structure defines the space. Flow defines what moves through it.
