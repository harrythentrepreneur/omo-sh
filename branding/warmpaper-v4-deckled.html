Typography

Perfect order is a lie. Breaking is how you prove it was yours.
Yifan (逸凡) — free and unspirited. The Deckled variation adds the texture of handmade paper — subtle grain across the page, rough edges on the type sample, and a warm paper shadow that lifts the text off the stock. Crimson Text carries the headlines with letterpress weight; IBM Plex Mono labels keep the brutalist precision. This is ink pressed into fiber.
Headlines: Crimson Text (serif)  |  Labels: IBM Plex Mono  |  Handmade paper stock.

Style Rationale

The Deckled direction pushes the Warm Paper concept all the way to the substrate. A subtle SVG noise texture — fractal turbulence at very low opacity — gives the background the feel of cotton rag paper. The type sample gets a warm paper shadow and rough, deckled edges via repeating SVG border patterns. Swatches carry micro-texture. Even the dark rationale block has a whisper of grain. This direction says: the manifesto was printed on a Heidelberg — but the paper was made by hand.
