Typography

Every line drawn has a reason. Nothing is decorative.
Like an architectural blueprint, this direction treats every element as load-bearing. The isometric O suggests three-dimensional structure — depth, weight, and deliberate construction. Drafting-blue accents appear only where they serve a function: crosshairs, callouts, a single dimension line. This is the brutalist spirit filtered through the discipline of the drafting table. No rendering. No shade. Just line work that carries intent.
All typography: IBM Plex Mono (300–700)  |  Accent: Drafting Blue #0050b0

Style Rationale

The Architect variation brings the precision of the drafting table into the brutalist frame. The logo is an isometric O — an axonometric volume rendered in pure line — evoking structural blueprints without ornament. The drafting grid behind the mark reinforces the idea that every pixel is measured. A single drafting-blue accent (#0050b0) appears only in functional elements: crosshairs, callout labels, section underlines. This direction says: we design like we build — with plans, not promises.
