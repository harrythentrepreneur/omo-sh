Style Rationale

The Warm Minimal direction is the closest to Omo's existing brand but pushes further into approachability. The logo mark fuses a speech bubble (voice) with a centered circle (presence), connected by a subtle arc to a spatial node — visually encoding all three pillars in one round, gentle mark. The color palette stays anchored in cream and warm brown but introduces terracotta as a secondary accent and pushes amber into a softer, honey-like range. Rounded corners, generous padding, and low contrast create a psychologically safe brand feel — important for a research lab asking people to trust agents with their attention and presence. This direction says: the future of computing is kind.
