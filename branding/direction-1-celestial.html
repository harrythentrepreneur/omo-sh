Typography

Voice, Space, Presence — all three at once.
Omo Research builds working prototypes that explore how human beings will communicate with AI agents. Not a hypothetical paper. Not a speculative deck. Deployed, open-source, running on the hardware you already carry. The next interface is ambient, spatial, and speaks your language.
Headings: Space Grotesk (300–700)  |  UI / Labels: JetBrains Mono (400–600)
System fallbacks: system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif

Style Rationale

The Celestial / Orbital direction draws a deliberate parallel between Omo Research's three pillars — voice, space, and presence — and the mechanics of orbital systems. The central planet represents presence (the agent's grounded awareness), while orbiting paths and a traveling node evoke voice in motion and spatial computing. Deep navy conveys scientific rigor and the unknown frontier; warm gold provides the human warmth that Omo's personality demands. The central dot — an "eye" inside the planet — hints at attention and awareness, the unspoken fourth dimension of agent communication. This direction says: we are doing serious research, but there is wonder in it.
