Typography

Perfect order is a lie. Breaking is how you prove it was yours.
Yifan (逸凡) — free and unspirited. The Warm Paper variation trades cold black for deep brown ink on warm off-white stock. Crimson Text carries the headlines with letterpress weight; IBM Plex Mono labels keep the brutalist precision. The tension feels handmade — like a manifesto pulled from a Heidelberg press.
Headlines: Crimson Text (serif)  |  Labels: IBM Plex Mono  |  Ink on paper.

Style Rationale

The Warm Paper direction recasts the Yifan Unbound concept in letterpress tones — deep brown ink on warm off-white stock. The fracturing double-frame icon remains structurally identical but rendered in taupes and ink-browns that recall pre-digital printing. Crimson Text headlines evoke the gravity of a manifesto, while IBM Plex Mono labels preserve the brutalist precision. This direction says: the break is not digital — it is physical, it is ink, it is pressure on paper.
