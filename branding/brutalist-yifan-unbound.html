Typography

Perfect order is a lie. Breaking is how you prove it was yours.
Yifan (逸凡) — free and unspirited. The Unbound variation is the most restrained of the three: pure black and white with five structural grays, no accent color at all. The tension itself becomes the color. The logo shows the double-frame split down the middle — the left half is pristine, sharp, immaculate brutalist geometry. The right half fractures: frame lines scatter into segments, the "O" circle breaks into detached rectangles, and a single piece floats upward, escaped.
All typography: IBM Plex Mono (300–700)  |  Pure B&W. Tension is the color.

Style Rationale

The Yifan Unbound direction is the most quietly radical of the three — it introduces no accent color, using only the black-and-white palette with five structural grays inherited from the master brutalist direction. Instead, the concept itself carries the energy. The logo presents the same double-frame structure as the original but splits it down the middle: the left half is pristine brutalist geometry — heavy, sharp, immaculate. The right half is actively fracturing — frame lines break into scattered segments, the "O" circle disintegrates into detached rectangles, and one piece floats upward on dashed motion lines. The center holds a solid anchor square. This direction says: 守破離 (shu-ha-ri) — first obey the form, then break it, then transcend it entirely.
