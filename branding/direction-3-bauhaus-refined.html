Style Rationale

The Bauhaus Refined direction takes the geometric abstraction already present in Omo's design language and pushes it further. Where the existing brand hints at Bauhaus influence through warm minimalism, this direction fully commits to the movement's core vocabulary: primary colors (red, blue, yellow) anchored on a cream base, pure geometry (circles, rectangles, triangles) composing a unified mark, and structural clarity through intentional negative space. The interlocking shapes in the logo form "omo" without spelling it — the "m" is blue verticals bridged by yellow triangles; the twin "o"s are red circles with cream cutouts. This direction says: research is a tradition of disciplined exploration — and we are extending that tradition into the age of agents.
