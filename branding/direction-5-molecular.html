Typography

Every agent interaction is a molecular bond between three forces.
Omo Research maps the architecture of agent communication. Voice carries the signal. Space provides the topology. Presence anchors the interaction in time and attention. Each is a node; the bonds between them are the protocol we're building. We work in code, not in decks. We ship prototypes, not papers. The result is an open-source stack that turns agent communication from a research problem into a working interface.

Style Rationale

The Molecular direction visualizes Omo's three pillars — voice, space, and presence — as connected nodes in a molecular structure. The logo arranges three circular nodes in a deliberate triangle: voice at the top (teal, with sound wave arcs), presence at the center (amber, the largest and most grounded), and space at the bottom-right (teal, with coordinate marks). Bonds connect the nodes — solid lines for direct relationships, a dashed arc for the indirect voice↔space path that presence mediates. A subtle dot-grid background evokes the laboratory / biotech research aesthetic. The dark-slate palette with teal as the primary signal color and amber as the presence anchor says: this is rigorous, scientific work — but the structure is beautiful.
