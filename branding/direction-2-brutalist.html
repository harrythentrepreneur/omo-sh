We build working prototypes, not slide decks.
Omo Research ships code. Open-source. Runs on the hardware you already own. Voice. Space. Presence. Three vectors, one protocol. We don't write about the future of agent interfaces — we deploy them and watch what breaks. Then we fix it. Then we ship again. No AI-marketing jargon. No hypothetical roadmaps. Just working software and honest research notes.
All typography: IBM Plex Mono (300–700)  |  No secondary font.

Style Rationale

The Brutalist direction is an act of intentional reduction. No gradients. No softness. No AI-marketing sheen. The monogram — a stark OR framed in heavy black lines with a disruptive diagonal slash — communicates what Omo actually does: cut through the noise. The monospace-only typography removes the distance between code and brand; what you see in the README is what you see on the site. The palette is unapologetically binary — black and white with a few structural grays — echoing the underground zine, xAI, and raw research preprint aesthetic. This direction says: we are too busy shipping to polish the logo. Read the source.
