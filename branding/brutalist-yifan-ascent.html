Typography

Some things don't fit inside the border anymore.
Yifan (逸凡) — free, unspirited, transcending restraint. The Ascent variation encodes breakthrough into the brutalist frame itself. The logo mark shows wings built from sharp geometric blades punching through a double-border that was never meant to contain flight. Vermillion marks only the rupture point — the single moment where motion overcomes structure. This is the brutalist spirit of build and break upward.
All typography: IBM Plex Mono (300–700)  |  Accent: Vermillion #cc2200

Style Rationale

The Yifan Ascent direction applies the concept of 逸凡 — free and unspirited — to the brutalist frame as an act of controlled rupture. The double-border, inherited from the master brutalist direction, is deliberately broken at the top. Geometric wing-forms ascend through the gap, built from hard triangular blades that retain the stark line vocabulary of the reference directions. A single vermillion (#cc2200) marks the breakthrough — used only at the point of rupture and nowhere else. The "O" mark opens upward into the ascent rather than closing as a circle. The palette is otherwise pure B&W with the standard five structural grays. This direction says: we build within the frame until the frame is too small — then we break it.
