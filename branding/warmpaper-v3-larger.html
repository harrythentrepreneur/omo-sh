Typography

Perfect order is a lie. Breaking is how you prove it was yours.
Yifan (逸凡) — free and unspirited. The Larger variation scales everything up for poster presence. The fracturing icon grows bolder; the headlines fill the frame with letterpress authority. Same warm paper, same deep ink — now at the scale of a manifesto on the wall.
Headlines: Crimson Text (serif)  |  Labels: IBM Plex Mono  |  Poster scale.

Style Rationale

The Larger direction takes the Warm Paper DNA and blows it up to poster proportions. Type scales up across the board — headlines at clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5rem), body copy at 1.05rem, generous whitespace throughout. The SVG icon grows to 200px with bolder strokes (10pt outer, 4pt inner) so the fracture reads from across the room. This direction says: the manifesto doesn't just sit on a page — it commands the wall.
