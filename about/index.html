Research log

Everything we built, tried or wrote down on the way to Omo Space, in order. 51 entries; click any one for more. Some lasted a day.

  1. Apr 2026Talk to anythingPoint a phone at an object, tap it, and talk to it. A hackathon build.
  2. Apr 2026A voice loop with captionsSpeak, see what it heard, hear the answer. The earliest voice interface.
  3. Apr 2026A desk with a hologramNotes on a main desk, a big monitor of live data and a hologram in the corner.
  4. May 2026Holographic co-founderA voice-first hologram with charts in the air and a pixel office of agents. Third place at a hackathon.
  5. May 2026Voice-only controlRunning email, ads, files and agents by voice alone, on paper first.
  6. May 2026Smart glasses, on paper“One pair, every environment”: on-device AI glasses and a small vision model for wearables.
  7. Jun 2026The Omo ShipA vision document: a ship as the place where agents live and work.
  8. Jun 2026Research into the next interfaceWriting down the question that became Omo Research.
  9. Jun–Aug 2026Agents in a Minecraft worldAgents as characters with homes, banners and screens. The world is the interface.
  10. Jul 2026omo.shThe site: “The next interface isn't a chat window.”
  11. Jul 2026Research postsA series of image posts: the world is the interface; voice, space, presence.
  12. Jul 2026A virtual office as the default interfaceA game office as the main way to work with agents, instead of a messaging app.
  13. Sep 2026The official desktop appGot it installed, looked for a task board in it.
  14. Sep 2026Terminal workspaceOne space per project, one tab per agent. Felt magical until copying over SSH broke.
  15. Sep 2026Chat server as HQA channel per agent, reasoning and tools visible, an HQ that sends work out and relays results.
  16. Sep 2026One chat that runs everythingTalk to one agent, it delegates and reports back. Hard to tell who was answering.
  17. Sep 2026A terminal channelSyncing the terminal chat and the chat app into one conversation.
  18. Sep 2026Kanban boardsA web board and an always-on native window. The window was retired.
  19. Sep 2026Browser extensionA community-built side panel next to the page. We tried it.
  20. Sep 2026Status dashboardLogs, services and costs in one window.
  21. Sep 2026Other people's setupsCompared our setup with other builders' agent personas and stewardship flows.
  22. Sep 2026A profile-pool forkDelegation that routes work to specialist agent profiles.
  23. Sep 2026Minecraft with the best modsShaders, distant horizons, and fixing a ghosting bug one mod at a time.
  24. Sep 2026A computer inside MinecraftStarted from a toy in-game computer, then replaced it with a real terminal.
  25. Sep 2026A pocket computerThe terminal as an item you carry and type into.
  26. Sep 2026Web pages on monitorsReal web pages on in-game walls that stay where you put them.
  27. 16 Sep 2026A product spec for computers in MinecraftComputer block, terminal block, persistent web monitors.
  28. Sep 2026Our own launcherOur own launcher instead of a third-party one, in our own style.
  29. Sep 2026A workspace mapA drawn map of every room in the chat server and how work moves between them.
