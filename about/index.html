01 / Questions

What we want to understand

These are directions for inquiry, not claims that the work is complete.

Voice

When does an agent feel present?

How do timing, interruption, tone, and turn-taking change an agent from a tool you command into something you can think alongside?

Space

Can place replace the menu?

Could rooms, distance, movement, and orientation make complex agent systems easier to understand than tabs, lists, and chat threads?

Agency

What should remain local?

Which interactions become more private, resilient, and immediate when perception and inference happen on the hardware around us?

02 / Method

How a question becomes research

01

Frame a question

Define the human behavior or interaction we want to understand—not a feature we want to justify.

02

Build the smallest environment

Create a focused prototype that makes the question observable in use.

03

Use it in the world

Document what feels natural, what breaks, and where our assumptions were wrong.

04

Share what we learn

Document the prototype, method, failures, and conclusions so the work can be challenged and extended.
