01 / Directions

What we want to make possible

These are interface directions—not product announcements or promises.

Direction

Agents you can speak with

Conversation that respects interruption, silence, rhythm, and attention instead of forcing every thought through a text box.

Direction

Work you can move through

Spatial interfaces where place and proximity explain what an agent knows, what it can do, and when it should be present.

Direction

Intelligence that stays close

Smaller, focused systems that run near the person using them and remain useful without a permanent cloud connection.

02 / Principles

The bar for becoming a product

01

Felt in the first minute

The value should be understandable through use, without a tour of the technology underneath it.

02

Calm enough to live with

Presence should not become noise. The interface must know when to recede.

03

Clear about its limits

People should understand what the system knows, what it can act on, and what remains uncertain.

04

Built to leave

Data and workflows should remain portable. A useful product should not depend on captivity.

03 / Releases

No public releases yet
